# Limitations and Disclosure Boundary

Brain AI is an experimental research architecture.

## Not established

The currently disclosed evidence does not establish:

- AGI or human-level general intelligence,
- a literal biological brain simulation,
- formal correctness of the complete architecture,
- superiority over conventional agents on real-world task suites,
- production-scale distributed reliability,
- immunity to prompt injection,
- immunity to host or dependency compromise,
- universal performance under distribution shift.

## Simulation boundary

The disclosed **0.764% false-resolution rate** is specific to the tested R-013 simulator and candidate. Real evidence sources may exhibit correlations, adversarial behavior, bias, drift, or failure modes not represented in that simulation.

## Private research boundary

Later research directions and security architecture are intentionally omitted from this public repository until they are either safe to disclose or have reached an appropriate validation state.

## Why the boundary matters

A security-oriented AI architecture should not undermine its own security by publishing the exact controls, thresholds, attack assumptions, and bypass-sensitive implementation details that protect it.
